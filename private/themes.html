Admin Screener

Add stocks to the screener, and keep the themes that group them. A stock belongs to the screener on its own — a theme is optional grouping, and a stock can sit in several. To put one stock into a theme, the star on its row in the screener is quicker than anything here.

Remove many from the screener

This takes the stocks out of the screener and every theme, and deletes their stored data. Bars and earnings can be re-pulled for a credit; recorded fundamentals cannot — that series starts from zero if you add the ticker back. The themes

Click a name to rename it. Drag a row, or use the arrows, to set the order — that order is the order everywhere. Colours follow the theme's name, so reordering never repaints them.
