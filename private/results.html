Earnings results, read from the press releases

Every quarterly results release a company we track has filed with the SEC in the last three weeks. Revenue, net income, earnings per share and guidance come straight from the company's own press release (Exhibit 99.1 to its 8-K), with the change on the same quarter a year earlier. Each release is linked, so every figure can be checked at the source.

How these figures are read

An AI model (Claude Haiku) reads each release and copies the figures as printed. Our own code then checks every figure against the release text and refuses any it cannot find there, or any whose size does not fit the company. A refused figure is shown as a dash rather than guessed.

The adjusted EPS is the company's own non-GAAP figure, under the company's own name for it. Guidance is shown in the words the company used. Companies define revenue and adjusted earnings differently, so compare a company with its own past rather than with another company.

Machine reading can still be wrong. Check the release before relying on a figure. Nothing here is investment advice, and nothing here says whether to trade any stock.
