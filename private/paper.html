Paper positions

Practise with pretend money: $100,000 to start, or an amount you choose. Open a position in any stock the site tracks and close it when you choose; a fill today is the latest price the site holds, which refreshes every half hour while the market is open. You can also date a position in the past, up to about four years back, at the price you paid on that day, to record positions you already hold. Only you can see this account. It is a simulation, not advice, and no real money or order is involved.

Open a position

For a past date, enter the price you paid per share and the shares as they appear on your statement; leave the price blank to use that day's close. The price must be within the day's low and high. If the stock has split since, your figures are converted to today's basis and both are kept. The Signal is not recorded for past dates.
