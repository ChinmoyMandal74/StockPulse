For every S&P 500 company, a reporting season at a time: the fundamentals the Signal reads from Twelve Data, beside the same figures from the company's own results press release as read by the AI model. The Signal reads Twelve Data's figures only; this page checks them. Each season's Twelve Data figures are kept once Twelve Data reaches the same report, so a past season shows what described it.

Reading the recon…

Revenue growth and earnings growth are the quarter against the same quarter a year earlier on both sides (Twelve Data's earnings growth is net income's), so they should agree; the gap is in points and amber past the tolerance. Profit margin and net income are twelve months at Twelve Data and one quarter in the release, so they are set side by side and never called a match. FCF margin at Twelve Data is levered free cash flow, a different measure from the releases’ (see Twelve Data and the releases). Provider behind: Twelve Data's last report date is not within five days of the release, so its figures still describe the quarter before (shown grey). A bank's revenue can be a different line on the two sides (net revenue against total revenue). Status: Agrees, Differs, Provider behind, Incomplete (nothing both sides carry), No release yet.
