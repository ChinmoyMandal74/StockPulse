Mobile setup

The views the phone offers, and what each row shows under the price. The mobile page has no settings of its own — someone on a phone only picks between these. Up to six views, up to five fields each; a phone row fits about four comfortably.

How a row will look

Everyone on a phone sees these views, guests included, and their choice of view is remembered on their own device. The values are formatted exactly as the screener's hover card formats them — the same descriptions, so a number cannot read differently in the two places. Deleting the last view puts the built-in three back.
