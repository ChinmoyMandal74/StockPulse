The shape of it

Every figure in this section is counted from the record below, so pressing Refresh keeps all of them true. Nothing here is typed in by hand.

The pace

One bar per day. A faint bar is a day with no changes at all — weekends, mostly. The ramp is the real story: the first week carried two changes and the fourth carried over a hundred, because the early work was building the floor everything later stands on.

The chapters

The record below is complete but relentless, so this is the arc through it. The dates and the writing are mine; the count beside each chapter is counted from the live record, so if a chapter's dates ever stop matching the work, its number will say so rather than the page quietly glossing over it.

Every change

Newest first, a week at a time. Each entry is the one-line summary the change was filed under, then the opening of the explanation that came with it. The six characters on the right open the full record on GitHub, including the code itself.

What this cannot tell you

  • A note written at the time says what was intended, which is not always what the reader ended up with. Several entries below are corrections of an earlier entry, and a few correct a correction.
  • The record starts at the first change and not before it. Whatever thinking happened before that first day left no trace here.
  • The failures are mostly invisible. Ten research ideas were tested against twenty years of market data and nine of them came back flat — and a result that does not work ships nothing, so it appears here as a single entry recording the measurement rather than as the days it took. The same is true of one feature built over roughly forty changes and then deleted when it was measured and found to predict nothing.
  • A day is the day the work was done, not the clock in London. Dates are grouped in New York time, because that is where the work happened and where the market this screens is open. Grouping by universal time had reported a fifty-change day that never happened.
  • Lines of code are not in here, and deliberately. Counting them rewards writing more of them; several entries below are worth more than the rest and delete things.
