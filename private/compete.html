Competitions

Paper positions as a competition. Each team gets one shared pretend account, with the same starting amount and the same days as every other team. Enter on your own, start a team and share its code with up to four others, or join a team with its code. Teams open and close positions at the latest price the site holds while the competition runs, and are placed by return. When the last day closes, the standings are final and the highest return wins. Other members see each team's name, its members' chosen names and its return. They also see its positions, unless the team chooses to hide them. A simulation with pretend money: not advice, and no prizes.
