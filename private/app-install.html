The Tickr Lab app

Tickr Lab on your phone’s home screen: the ready-made screens, every stock the site tracks with its Signal and readings, and, if you turn them on, a notification when one of your price alerts fires or a new post goes up. It is for members, and free.

On an iPhone

  1. Open tickrlab.com/m in Safari and sign in.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Tickr Lab from your home screen. For notifications, tap the bell at the top.

On Android

  1. Open tickrlab.com/m in Chrome and sign in.
  2. Tap Install when it is offered, or the menu (⋮) and Add to Home screen.
Not a member yet? Create a free account first; the app opens with your sign-in. Notifications are only what you choose: your own alerts, new posts, or neither, and they can be turned off from the bell or the phone’s settings.
