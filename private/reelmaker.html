1 · Images, up to four

The images stay in this browser: nothing is uploaded or stored. A caption is optional, typed onto the image as it arrives; keep it short, most Reels are watched with the sound off.

2 · Shape

Instagram posts every new video as a Reel. A 9:16 video fills the Reels screen; a 4:5 or square one plays at its own shape in the feed and with space around it in the Reels screen. An image of a different shape sits whole in the middle, over a blurred copy of itself, so nothing is cut off.
