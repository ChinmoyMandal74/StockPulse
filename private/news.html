Article tone

News

Every article in Yahoo Finance's feed for every company on the screener, read by an AI model that says what kind of article it is, how its content leans towards the company, whether it reports a new company event, and sums it up in two sentences. A day at a time, the most important first. The same story told by several outlets is shown once, with the others under it.

 
Reading the day…

Importance is a rule, not a rating. High: the article is mainly about the company and reports a company event, such as results, guidance, a deal, a legal or regulatory decision or a leadership change. Medium: an analyst action, a product or customer, a dividend or buyback, an insider trade or a holdings filing. Low: opinion, a price recap, an industry piece, and any article that only mentions the company. A high-kind article that reports no new event (a preview, commentary on a deal) counts as medium; a story told by four or more outlets that day rises one level. Tone runs from −1 to +1 and describes the article's content, not the stock. Nothing here is a recommendation, and none of it feeds the Signal. Headlines are the publishers' own; follow the link to read the article.
