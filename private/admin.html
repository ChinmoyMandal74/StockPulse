Scoring

How momentum is weighted on the screener.

Momentum weighting

This changes the Momentum and Overall columns on the screener and on /analysis, and therefore the sort — the two score columns are tinted while a non-default weighting is in force. The nightly email, the assistant and the stored snapshot always stay on Default, so the shared score remains one comparable measurement. What does each factor mean?

Reference data

Other people's lists, kept at arm's length from ours.

NASDAQ listings Every US-listed company with a market cap beside it — free, keyless, and joined to nothing. Where to go looking for a candidate ticker.

Twelve Data will not sell this on the current plan at a sane price: /market_cap is Ultra-only and /statistics is 50 credits a symbol, so sweeping NYSE and NASDAQ would be about 287,000 credits and eight hours. NASDAQ publishes the same thing for nothing. It lives in its own table on its own page and is never joined to the screener — its sector and industry are a third taxonomy, and its market cap is a different vendor's snapshot on a different clock.
