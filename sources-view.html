Recommended next: E14, work-visa filings: exact matches by tax ID onto the retirement-plan data, wages and job titles that no current source has, and from the Labor Department, so no risk to the SEC access. Then E8 bank call reports, the quickest full set of accounts.
Unfinished: the federal contracts download for fiscal 2016–24 (half done, resumes run by run), then its load; the slow SEC reads (acquired-company accounts for Continental's peers, then Form C and Reg A).
The gap to be honest about: most small private companies never raise registered money or get bought by a filer, so for them the free sources give headcount and government work at best. That is why the paid tools exist; the edge here is the free sources joined to the listed peers the app already covers well.
Rules for every loader: bulk files only, one request at a time with a long pause, a daily cap, any refusal stops the run and nothing is retried, nothing fetched twice. SEC files with the declared user agent (the address has had a 429 and a 403). People's names and roles kept, never their street addresses. The full notes are in docs/backlog.md, entry 61.
