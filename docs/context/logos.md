## Logos — built and removed the same day
**There are no company logos anywhere (owner's call, 2026-09-14): the provider's marks aged badly and sat on white boxes, and the whole pipeline came out the day it went in** — the `logos` table (dropped from Turso), the fetch/override/serving routes, the admin button, its receipt email, the LogoFX transparency module, and every `<img>`. Tickers render as **typographic monograms** (`.lg` on the promo cards) or plain text; the stock masthead is back to the symbol alone. What the episode measured, kept for the next time logos come up: Twelve Data `/logo` costs 1 credit/symbol and its image URL is keyless (~7KB 128px JPEG) but the marks lag rebrands by years; Wikimedia thumbnails moved to `thumb.wikimedia.org`, exist only at pre-rendered sizes, and require a User-Agent; ~11 of 93 symbols (ETFs mostly) had no mark at all. If logos ever return, the per-symbol URL override was the workable shape and the bulk provider was the problem.
