# The Stock by stock card (2026-11-05)

The owner, after the post of eight AI themes on eight slides: "Sometimes it's hard to read a lot of data on one slide .. one overview and one page per stock .. show the text bigger size". A one-theme carousel was then built by hand for AI - Memory, and the owner chose to have it in the studio ("Let's do 1").

- **Template `thmsbs`**, under **Theme** as "Stock by stock (carousel)". Three controls: `tsbName` (the theme, an entity picker, so nothing draws until one is chosen), `tsbCount` (up to 3 to 9 companies, largest by market value first; default nine) and `tsbSlide` (0 is the overview, 1 to 9 the companies). The slide values are fixed and only their labels are renamed for the theme (`relabelTsbSlides`), so a saved post keeps its slide; a slide past the theme's last company is hidden and falls back to the overview.
- **Nine at most** because an Instagram carousel is ten pictures: the overview and nine companies. A theme with more says "The 9 largest of 18 by market value" on the overview.
- **Add every slide** (`igAllBtn`, under Post to Instagram, admin only like the rest of that box): draws and stores each slide in order into the tray, waiting for the price history if it is still on its way, then puts back the slide that was showing. It refuses when the tray would pass ten.

**What each slide shows**
- Overview: the theme, how many fell on the day, the median day's move and median distance from the 52-week high, then a row a company: the day's move, from the high, revenue growth, and forward P/E at the high and now.
- A company: its name and ticker, price and day's move, a year of its price (the house `lineChart`, rebased) with the high marked (`opts.peak`: a dot, its price and day, a dashed level and a drop to the last close), and six tiles: from the 52-week high, revenue growth, P/E at the high and now, forward P/E at the high and now, estimated earnings over the last twelve months (P/E over forward P/E), market value.

**How the figures are worked** (`sbsFigures`)
- From the high is the screener's `pctFromHigh` (intraday highs over 252 sessions); the high's price is `price / (1 + pctFromHigh/100)` and its day is `daysSince52wHigh` sessions back on the basket's axis. A history shorter than the window (a recent listing: SK hynix's US shares from 2026-07-10) reads "from its high since Jul 10". A high in an earlier year than the latest session carries its year.
- P/E is the price over diluted EPS, a dash off a loss. **"At the high"** is the high's price over TODAY's earnings and estimates: the stored ratios begin 2026-08-30, after most highs, so the fall alone moves it. Every slide says so.
- **The forward P/E is repriced to the latest close.** The provider's ratio is struck at the price on the day the profile was pulled (`profileFetchedAt`), up to a week before under the rotation; on a day a theme falls 15% that overstates it by as much. The card takes the session the pull priced at (the pull's own day if after 4 p.m. New York, else the session before) and multiplies by the latest close over that close, both read off the basket. A pull outside the year gives a dash.
- **To the basket's precision**: `/api/basket` rounds each series to 3dp, so a repriced multiple can differ from one worked off raw closes by one unit in its last digit (Seagate 19.0x against 19.1x). The Chart card's notes record the same rounding.
- Revenue growth is `revenueGrowthYoY`, the latest quarter on a year earlier (the source rule's, as on the screener).
- Colour: green and red on the day's move only; the high and the distance from it are orange; the price line is the accent.

**Plumbing**: `Cards.basketDays('thmsbs')` is a year; the card names its own stocks to `getBasket` (one fetch for every slide), and `/api/m/post` makes the same dry build to ask for them (as for the race and chart cards). `Cards.sbsSlides` and `Cards.sbsMembers` tell the studio how many slides and whose. In `FILL_TPLS`, so the chart grows into spare room.

**Test** (scratchpad `sbs-test.js`, the real server on an in-memory database with production's Nov 5 snapshot, its themes and a year of bars from the local copy): offered under Theme; three themes (Memory; Power Generation, eighteen companies; Neoclouds, mostly loss-making) at all three sizes, every slide: nothing past the artboard, the note above the footer, no figure clipped, green and red on the day's move only; on 4:5 each company's figures against a working from the raw closes, the high's price and day against the raw bars, and the overview's order and count; Micron's forward P/E against the figure posted the same day from the fundamentals history (6.0x to 3.1x); Add every slide fills the tray with seven and puts slide 3 back; a saved post drawn by the server with Micron's repriced forward P/E. 287 checks. Proved by reverting: no repricing, no year on an old high, slides past the last company left showing.
