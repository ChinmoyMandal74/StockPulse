## Paper positions — a pretend account per member (2026-10-15, owner's request)

The owner: *"Can we add mock trade feature"*, then *"go ahead"* on the proposal with its defaults: open and close (never buy and sell), $100,000 to start with a reset, dollar amounts allowed, members and admin only, the owner able to review.

### What it is
- **`/paper`**, members and admin (guests redirected; `/paper.html` redirects too). Reached from the screener menu (Paper positions) and a **Paper position** pill on each stock page (`/paper?open=SYM`, shown once `/api/me` says not a guest).
- Four totals (account value, return so far, the S&P 500 over the same days, cash), a form to open a position by dollars or shares, a chart of the account at each close against SPY bought with the same $100,000 on the start day, open positions with a Close row (part or all), closed trades, and **Start again** (confirmed; deletes the ledger, restarts today).
- **The wording rule holds**: a position is opened and closed; nothing says buy or sell. The page and the footer say it is a simulation with pretend money and not advice. The Signal on the day a position was opened is kept and shown, canonical words via `Action.canon`; the Signal reads nothing from here.

### How it works
- **A fill is the snapshot's price** with its `pricedAt` (the half-hourly intraday refresh, or the last close outside market hours). Nothing can be entered at a past price.
- **The ledger is the truth.** `paper_trades` is append-only, one row per open or close (`ts`, `kind`, `symbol`, `shares`, `price`, `priced_at`, `signal`, `note`), indexed `(user_key, id)`. `paper_accounts` holds `start_cash` and `started_at`. Everything else is worked out by **`paper.js`** (pure): `summarise` replays the ledger at **average cost** (a part-close takes its share of the cost; reopening after a full close starts a fresh average and open date), `sizeOpen` / `sizeClose` validate, `history` values the account at each SPY close from the start day, carrying a stock at its last close, trading days in New York time (`nyDay`).
- **Routes** (`requireMember`, keyed by `prefsKey`): `GET /api/paper` (summary, history, the picker list), `POST /api/paper/open` `{symbol, dollars | shares}`, `POST /api/paper/close` `{symbol, shares | all}`, `POST /api/paper/reset` `{confirm: true}`. Each write is one insert, never retried; reset is one batch. Activity log records `open:SYM`, `close:SYM`, `reset`, never amounts. Cap: 5,000 trades per account.
- **History reads** are one indexed seek per symbol ever held plus SPY, bounded to the days since the start (`readBars`, limit about three quarters of the calendar days); none past 1,500 days.
- **A stock that leaves the universe** stays in the account, valued at its last traded price and marked "no longer tracked"; closing it fills there and the closed row says why. `paper_trades` is deliberately **not** in `SYMBOL_TABLES`: removing a stock must not erase a member's history.
- **Account deletion** removes both tables' rows (`deleteUser`). `/privacy` lists paper positions under What you create, deletion, and what the owner can see.
- **Owner's review**: `/api/users` attaches a `paper` summary per account (value, return, cash, open symbols, closed count), shown as the **Paper** column on `/users` with the detail in the tooltip.

### Known limits (said on the page where it matters)
- Price only: no dividends, fees or tax. No short positions, options or margin.
- Two opens sent at the same instant could each pass the cash check; the button is disabled while a request is in flight, and it can only overspend the member's own pretend cash.
- The chart's last point is the latest stored close; the totals above it use the latest price, so they can differ intraday.

### Dated positions and a starting amount (2026-10-20)
The owner: "Is it possible to enhance the paper position to include entry date so that I can input historical purchases". Offered the choices, they took **B and B**: the price actually paid (checked against the day's range) rather than the day's close, and a starting amount of their own rather than a fixed $100,000. This **reverses** the founding rule "nothing can be entered at a past price", for dates in the past only. A fill dated today is still the live price and ignores any price typed.

- **Open and Close take an optional `date`** (the form's date field; the close row has its own) and, for a past date, a **price paid** (blank takes that day's close).
  - The day must be a stored trading day for the stock (`readBarOn`, a primary-key seek), within `MAX_BACK_DAYS` (1,460, the depth the account chart reads), and not in the future.
  - The price must lie in that day's low to high, with 0.5% of room for rounding in the adjusted bars (`Paper.pricedOn`); a refusal names the range.
  - Size is by dollars (at the price) or by shares.
- **Splits.** Stored bars are split-adjusted, so a statement from before a split is on another footing. `Paper.splitFactor` multiplies the stored splits after the day (`f` is the shares multiplier, so 10 for a 10-for-1; a purchase on the split day itself is already on the new basis). Shares entered are multiplied by it and the price divided; any price check is said on the statement's basis. The ledger keeps today's basis in `shares` and `price`, and the statement's figures in `stmt_shares` and `stmt_price` with `split_k`. A position built from such an entry is marked **split-adjusted** on the page.
- **The ledger stays the truth, replayed by day.** New columns on `paper_trades`: `d` (the trading day a trade is dated to; null is the New York day it was made), `stmt_shares`, `stmt_price`, `split_k` (`ADDED_COLUMNS`). `summarise` and `history` order by (day, entry order), `Paper.dayOf` and `whenOf`, so a past entry takes its place wherever it was typed in. **Every dated entry is checked against the whole ledger** (`Paper.checkLedger`): in day order the cash may never go below zero and a close may never take more than was held that day. An earlier open that would leave a later one short of cash is refused, naming the later day. `Paper.heldOn` gives what was open at the end of a day, for a dated close of all.
- **The account's start moves back** to the first dated day (`setPaperStartNoLater`, `min()` so never forward). The chart and the S&P comparison run from there.
- **The Signal is left blank for a dated entry** ("Not recorded for past dates"). The daily verdict on a past day is not stored, and `tech_history` is a weekly all-technical reading, a different measure.
- **Start again** asks on the page (no `confirm()` dialog) for a starting amount, $1,000 to $10,000,000 (`Paper.startCashOf`, commas and `$` read). `POST /api/paper/reset {confirm, startCash}`. `GET /api/paper` now returns `limits` (today, the earliest date, the cash bounds).
- `/privacy` says the dates, entered figures and starting amount are kept. The activity log records `open:SYM:dated` / `close:SYM:dated`, never amounts.

## /users printed its own script as text (fixed 2026-10-21)
- The commit that added the Paper column (`f787eb9`) pasted 177 lines after `</html>` on `/users`: two copies of the rest of the file, which the browser drew as text under the table. The patch used `String.replace` with a plain replacement string, and the paper cell's `'cash $' + …` contains `$'`, which `replace` reads as "everything after the match". The real `paperCell` and `pfCell` inside the script were right; the file was cut at its first `</html>`. Patch scripts pass a function as the replacement (`s.replace(a, () => b)`) so `$` patterns in code are never expanded. Every other page has exactly one `</html>`.
- **The same column hid the Delete buttons** (owner, 2026-10-21: "I thought there was option to delete a user for admin"). With Themes and Paper the table outgrew the page's 1,000px, and `body { overflow-x: clip }` cut off the last column (Approve, Disable, Delete) with no way to scroll to it. The page is now 1,280px wide and the table scrolls inside its card (`.tbl > .core { overflow-x: auto }`). Checked with production-length addresses, a theme and a paper account: at 1400px every Delete is on screen (the old layout: all three off it); at 900px the card scrolls to them and the page does not scroll sideways.

## Paper competitions (2026-11-11)
The owner: "Can you add a concept of paper position teams and competition .. ranking .. winners etc". Asked first, because the project rule bans rankings and "winners": the owner chose **an exception for paper competitions only** (members' pretend accounts placed by return; stocks never ranked; no buy/sell wording), **the admin runs them**, **no prizes**, and on visibility: *"Let me team decide if they want to show the positions or not .. the default should be show"*. The exception is written into CLAUDE.md beside the rule.

### What it is
- **`/compete`** (members and admin; guests redirected; `/compete.html` redirects). Linked from the screener bar (Competitions, hidden for guests like Paper positions) and the paper page's bar.
- **The admin** sets one up on the page (name, first and last New York day, starting amount $1,000 to $10,000,000, at most 400 days) and may change its name and days until it ends; the first day stays once it has started, and the amount stays once a team has entered.
- **Members enter** until the last day: on their own (a team of one, named as they are shown), by starting a team (a name, 2 to 30 characters, unique in the competition, any case), or by a team's **invite code** (eight characters without 0/O/1/I, typed loosely). Five members a team at most, 500 teams a competition, one entry a competition. Each member gives the **name they are shown by** (2 to 24 characters, offered as the first word of their account name); an email is never shown. Leaving is allowed only before the first day; a team left empty goes with its untouched account.
- **A team trades one shared paper ledger**, the ordinary paper tables under the key `team:<id>`, so `paper.js` does all the arithmetic. Its account is **`/paper?team=<id>`**: the same page in a team mode (the team's name as the title, the competition in the lede, no dated entries, no Start again, opening and closing off before the first day and after the last). **Fills are live only**: a dated entry would let an entrant open at a price already known to have risen. Any member of the team can open and close; another member's team is 403. The member's own `/paper` account is separate and unchanged.
- **The standings** (`Compete.standings`): placed by return, highest first; returns equal to a hundredth of a percent share a place and the next skips. While running, valued at the latest prices; **after the last day, at that day's close** (`compEndPrices`: one seek per held stock with `closesBefore`), held per instance once worked out. **The winner**, once ended, is the best return **among entrants that opened at least one position** (an untouched account cannot win by sitting in cash); a tie shares it. Beside each competition: the S&P 500 (SPY) from the close before the first day to the latest price, or to the last day's close.
- **Positions shown** with their weights unless the team's **captain** (who started it; the earliest remaining member if they leave or delete their account) hides them; then others see the count only. A team always sees its own. The invite code is shown only to the team.

### Storage and the rules around it
- `paper_comps`, `paper_teams` (unique name per competition, case-blind; unique code), `paper_team_members` (primary key competition and member, so one entry each). Small tables. The ledger rows are in `paper_accounts` / `paper_trades`.
- **Account deletion**: a team the member was alone in is deleted with its ledger; a team with others keeps its ledger and passes the captaincy on. `/privacy` says so.
- Activity log: `compete` with `create:`, `edit:`, `enter:`, `join:`, `leave:`, `open:SYM`, `close:SYM`; never amounts or names.
- The live open and close are now shared helpers (`paperLiveOpen`, `paperLiveClose`) used by `/paper` and the team accounts.

### Known limits
- A fill uses the latest stored price, up to half an hour old in market hours; in a competition a member could act on news before the price refreshes. Said on the page as for /paper.
- A split during a competition: the ledger keeps the pre-split shares and the stored closes are split-adjusted, the same limit `/paper` has.
- No moderation of team or shown names beyond the character rules; the admin can see every entry in the database.

The traded-only rule proven by removing it (2 failures).
