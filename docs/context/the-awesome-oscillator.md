## The Awesome Oscillator — a chart pane and a screener column (2026-10-09, owner's request)
**An `AO` button on `/stock`'s chart, and an `Awesome Osc` column in the Trend group immediately after MACD.** Asked for from two TradingView screenshots; offered the pane alone or the pane plus a column, the owner took both. The reading is the 5-session average of each session's MIDPOINT, (high + low) / 2, less the 34-session average.

- **DISPLAY ONLY.** Nothing in `action.js` reads `aoPct` or `aoRising`; the suite scores every row with the field present, absent and lying and demands identical verdicts. It is not in `CHAT_FIELDS` either.
- **THE PANE AND THE COLUMN ARE COLOURED BY DIFFERENT THINGS, and each says which.** The pane follows the indicator's own convention: a bar is green where it is HIGHER than the bar before it and red where it is lower, so a green bar can hang below zero. Everywhere else on that chart green and red mean the price rose or fell, so the pane's caption states the rule (*green higher than the bar before, red lower*). The column is coloured by the SIGN, like MACD beside it, and its tooltip says whether the reading is higher or lower than the session before.
- **THE COLUMN IS A SHARE OF THE PRICE (`aoPct`), the pane is in dollars.** The raw figure is in price units, so a column of it would rank the screen by share price. On one stock's chart the raw figure is the right one and is what the caption and the crosshair print.
- **Midpoints, so `/api/history` sends `mids`** — one array (3dp), not highs and lows; where a bar has neither, the close stands in. An older server sends none and the page falls back to closes. `aoCalc(values)` in server.js does the same fallback per bar and returns null under 35 sessions.
- **`RowCard.aoSeries(mids)`** is the series, and the pane is a third band in `chartSVG` (`o.ao`, `o.aoPrev` for the bar before the first one in view), between RSI and volume, at the RSI band's height. Bars are measured from a drawn zero line and scaled to the largest reading in view.
- **Chart heights**: the pane adds 71 viewBox units at the same px-per-unit, so RSI + AO is **679px**, either one alone 577, neither 468. **`has-ao` is set only when a pane is actually drawn**: a listing under 34 sessions has no reading, and the taller box would stretch every other band.
- **Off by default, remembered per device** (`localStorage` `stock.ao`), like the three layers under the chart. RSI still defaults on.
- One `FIELD_SPEC` row (`trend|Awesome osc.`) carries the column's figure to the hover card, `/stock`, the tiles, the phone and `/compare`.
- Constants after the change: the Trend banner **5**, `PAD_SPAN` **107**, the error row **102**, the empty row **104**.
- **The column is blank until the next FULL round rebuilds the snapshot** (about half an hour in session); the pane works at once, since it is computed in the browser from the history.
- Verified: **57 checks** (`ao-test.js`) over six planted stocks — highs and lows set so every reading is computed independently, the close moved off the midpoint so a close-based version reads 4.83 where the answer is 8.05, one stock below zero AND rising, one with 30 sessions, one with no highs or lows. The pane is measured as drawn: one bar per session, every bar's colour against an independent series, bars on both sides of zero, a half-size bar at half height, the RSI and volume bands keeping their pixel height. **Proved by reverting seventeen times, every one load-bearing**: the header without its body cell fails 7, dollars instead of a share of the price 5, closes instead of midpoints 4, and the rest 1 to 3 each.
