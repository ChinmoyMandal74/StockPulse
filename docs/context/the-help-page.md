## The help page
`/help` explains the app to the people using it, so it is open to **any signed-in user** rather than admin-only. Reached from `Help` in the ⋯ menu, beside Contact, and from a link in the Weights menu itself — which is where the question actually arises.

- **SIXTEEN numbered sections behind a contents grid**, rewritten 2026-09-27 at the owner's instruction — *"update the help page with all the latest features, help only applies to users of the system not admin"*. Each ends with a link back to the contents, and `scroll-margin-top` clears the sticky island on an anchor jump. (It was thirteen; sixteen before that, until the three scoring sections went with the score they described on 2026-09-23.)
  - **THE `.adm` BADGE IS GONE, AND SO IS THE RULE FOR IT.** It marked owner-only features so members could see what the buttons they lacked did — but those buttons left a member's bar for `/admin` long ago, so the badge was documenting a console its readers cannot open. The sections it sat on were **rewritten around what a member actually gets** rather than deleted: the assistant section went entirely (it is admin-only since 2026-09-26), the rule ladder became *"every Advice cell carries the one rule that fired"* — which is still true for a member — and Cushion kept its column and lost its panel. The CSS rule went too: residue reads as intent, and the next person would badge something with it.
  - **Four sections added for features that had no home**: the group pages, the pivot, compare and alerts. Four more were brought up to date: the stock page gained peers, earnings history, the SEC EDGAR card and the two out-links; the table section gained views, the filter row, the clickable Sector/Industry/Size cells, the two clocks and the no-hover-on-touch rule; "Screens" became "Narrowing it down" and gained favourites and the five bar pickers; the account section gained Google sign-in, the guest preview and the blog list.
  - **Four claims had outlived their features and are named in the test so they cannot come back**: a **welcome email** that has not been sent since 2026-09-26 (this file already recorded fixing that copy once — it was still there), **"49 columns"** against ninety, the **owner-only refresh buttons**, and the assistant. **A help page rots silently**: nothing breaks, it just starts lying, which is why the suite asserts dead claims ABSENT as well as new ones present.
- Verified: **45 checks** — every contents entry resolving to a section, sections and entries in agreeing order, **numbered 1..n against their actual position** (the classic rot after an edit), contents titles agreeing with their headings, a way back from each, an anchor jump landing clear of the sticky island, no sideways scroll at 1400px or 390px, nothing under 12px, plus the 27 present-claims and 7 absent-claims above.
- **It is the manual, and there is deliberately only one (2026-09-17).** The owner asked for a user manual; `/help` already was one — signed-in rather than admin, reached from `Help` in the ⋯ menu — so the four features written after it were added to it rather than to a second page. A separate guide beside it would have drifted the way every other duplicated thing here has. The gaps were tiles (the switch, per-row, the shared range, and that Tile setup is site-wide), news (the column's two kinds of blank, the panel, the 12-hour ticker, Check now), the phone page (the four tabs, views, the chart toggle, and that a reload is what fetches new data), and cards (Download PNG at true size, and saved posts remembering controls rather than a picture). Three older sections were missing their back-to-contents link and got one. **No new links were added anywhere — the owner's call.** Verified in a browser: 16 contents entries resolving to 16 sections in agreeing order, every section reachable back, anchor jumps clearing the sticky island, no sideways scroll at 390px, no text under 12px.
- Written for someone with a hand on a control, not someone studying the model — every factor says what raising or lowering it changes, not only what it is. Owner-only features carry an `.adm` badge rather than being hidden, so members can see what the buttons they lack actually do.
- **It restates numbers that live in code** — the default weights, the 65/35 blend, the preset table. If those change, this page has to change with them; there is deliberately no attempt to generate it from `screens.js`, because the prose around each number matters more than the number.
- The caution about fitting a weighting to today's list lives at the bottom of the presets section, next to the thing that invites it.
