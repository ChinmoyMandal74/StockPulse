## Saved posts — the studio on a phone
**A studio card, named and kept, opened on a phone and saved to the camera roll (2026-09-16, owner's idea).** The workflow it exists for: set a card up on the desktop once, then on any day open it on a phone and post it to Instagram without touching a computer.

- **A post stores the CONTROLS, not a picture** — `{id, name, tpl, size, opts}`, the studio's control values by control id. Opening it tomorrow draws tomorrow's data, which is the whole point.
- **Site-wide and admin-curated** (owner's call), in `app_meta` under `promo_presets`, the Tile setup and Mobile setup pattern — these are the brand's posts, not a per-account scratchpad. `GET /api/promo-posts` is **member-only**, `PUT` admin. `cleanPosts()` rebuilds from scratch: template checked against `Cards.ids`, artboard against the three real sizes, up to `POSTS_MAX` 12. **Control ids are shape-checked, not listed** — the studio owns that list and restating it here would drift the first time a control was added; the card builder ignores an id it does not know, exactly as `cleanViews` leaves the column list to the screener.
- **NOT `/api/posts` — the blog owns that path**, registered higher up, and Express takes the first match: the first cut answered the blog's empty list and the studio showed "None yet" with no error anywhere. Renamed to `/api/promo-posts`. Nothing but running it finds this.
- **The phone lists posts by NAME and builds one only when it is opened** (owner's call: "I need to see the Preset name and when it click on it it should open the visualization"). `GET /api/m/posts` is the names — **319 bytes for three** — and `GET /api/m/post?id=` builds that one card from the snapshot as it stands, with `Cards.STYLE`. So the tab costs nothing, the archive read a chart card needs happens on the tap, and **opening a post is what makes it current**. A thumbnail shelf was built first and thrown away.
- **The phone would otherwise need the 1.3MB snapshot and the card module to draw its own** — the same reason `/api/m/screen` formats rows here. `cards.js` got the Node-loadable tail for it (`global.Cards`, and only `injectStyle()` ever touches the DOM).
- **Nothing is cached on the phone but three display choices** (`m.view`, `m.chart`, `m.range`), and there is no service worker — so a reload always hits the network and always brings the latest snapshot. That is the owner's stated rule for this page: **no poller, no restore-on-return; a refresh is how you get new data, and it must actually get it.** Pinned by a test that rewrites the snapshot and re-opens a post.
- **`basketPayload()` was lifted out of `/api/basket`** so the posts endpoint runs the same curve rules rather than a second copy. The studio always reads `name=All` and lets the card narrow it, so one basket per window covers every scope.
- **Motion is gone entirely (2026-09-22, owner's call: "remove all animate and replay feature from promo"), and the video export went first (2026-09-16, "remove the export video option everywhere").** The exporter rendered the animation frame by frame into a MediaRecorder; the Still / Animate / Replay controls outlived it as a preview for six days and have now followed. Removed with them: `Cards.MOTION` and every `.motion` rule and `@keyframes` in the injected stylesheet, `Cards.motion()` / `Cards.unfreeze()` / `MOTION_MS`, the `STAGGER` list, the number-counting helpers (`numberTargets` / `setNumbersAt`), and `Cards.freezeAt()` before them. **`pathLength="1"` went too** — it existed only so one dash rule could animate any line without measuring it, and an attribute whose sole purpose has been deleted is residue that reads as intent. The PNG export lost its unfreeze-then-restore dance, which only existed so a card caught mid-fade was not photographed at whatever opacity that frame happened to be on.
  - No saved post carried a motion setting — it was never in `CONTROL_IDS`, so `cleanPosts` and stored posts needed nothing. the phone never used it either; `promo.html` was the only host.
  - Verified: 13 checks driving the real studio — the three buttons gone, the word absent from the panel, nothing carrying the class, no `.motion` or `@keyframes` left in the injected stylesheet, the chart still drawing solid strokes with no leftover `pathLength`, and **Download PNG still producing a real image** (572 KB for a chart card), which is the one thing the removal could plausibly have broken.
  - Two fixture traps, both the same shape: the template picker is **`#tplSelect`**, and selecting `#tpl` inside a `.catch()` meant the template never changed, the chart never drew, and the stroke assertions passed over ZERO paths. And the chart card **reads the archive** — with no bars seeded it sits on its "Drawing the chart…" placeholder forever, which is the other way that test was passing over nothing.
- **Each saved post carries Replace and Delete as labelled buttons.** The delete was a bare `×` beside the word Replace and read as punctuation rather than as the other choice.
- **Clicking a post reloads the whole panel, and `render()` alone is not enough.** The template and size chips are painted by `paintControls()`; without it the card redrew while the chips stayed lit on whatever was chosen before, which reads as nothing having happened. `openPost` also sets every `*Sector` control first, calls `syncIndustries(true)`, then sets the rest — an industry only exists as an option once its sector is set, and depending on the order the keys happen to be stored in is not a plan. Pinned by a test that drives the real UI, saves a filtered card, moves template / shape / every control away, clicks the post and diffs all 43 controls plus both chips plus the card's own markup — and it was checked against the broken version first, where the two chip assertions fail.
- **`private/cardshot.js` is the rasteriser, and there were THREE copies of it** — promo.html, the since-deleted cards.html, and the phone would have been the third. Extracted: computed styles inlined onto a clone (a serialized SVG sees none of the page's stylesheets), the Google fonts fetched once and embedded as data URIs (an external ref inside an SVG-as-image is dropped, and a face still pointing at the network *taints the canvas*), then foreignObject → canvas at the card's true pixel size. Verified straight after the move: a real 1080×1350 PNG, 538 KB in 249ms, and the studio's own Download button still downloading.
- **The artboard's own styles moved into `cards.js` as `.s-art`**, for the same reason — promo.html and the since-deleted cards.html each had a copy, and the phone would have made three. Each host still owns what is genuinely its own: the studio scales it to the window, `/cards` fixes one shape and adds a drop shadow.
- **The phone's fourth tab, Posts** (hidden from guests: the studio is member-only, and these carry portfolio names and whole-universe rankings the preview withholds). Thumbnails are **the real card scaled**, not a reflowed copy — a 1080px artboard with `transform: scale(measured width / 1080)`, so the picture tapped is the picture saved; the scale is measured after layout rather than computed, so rotating the phone re-fits it. **The frame must be `display:block`** — it is a `<span>` inside a `<button>`, and its height was silently ignored until it was.
- **Tapping a name opens the card itself**, scaled to the sheet, with the data's timestamp on it; **Save as an image** is the second step, because rasterising 1080×1920 on a phone takes a moment and is only wanted when the file is. A screenshot would be the phone's aspect ratio with a status bar in it; the PNG is the exact artboard. The card is built off-screen at 1080 (`#shot`), shot, and removed; the object URL is released on `sheetclose`.
- **Two bugs a screenshot caught that passing assertions did not.** The PNG came out **perfectly sized and completely blank**: `inlineStyles` copies the original's own placement onto the clone, so the off-screen `left:-20000px` went with it and the card rendered outside the canvas — `shoot()` now neutralises the clone's position (to `relative`, not `static`: the card is the containing block for its own aura). And `.sheet h2` beat `.s-title` on specificity, quietly resetting the card's 66px headline to 18px — every sheet rule that a card could also match is a **child** selector now. The tests check ink and computed type, not just dimensions.
- Verified: 29 checks — validation (unknown template, unknown artboard, nameless, junk control ids including `__proto__`, the cap), every post built server-side with its artboard and the chart card actually reading the archive, the studio listing / opening / saving (template, size and controls all restored), the phone's tab and thumbnails (real card, scaled, right shape, no sideways scroll), a 1080×1350 and a 1080×1920 PNG rendered on the phone as blobs, the off-screen stage cleaned up; plus the role walk (guest 403 on both post routes, member read-only, owner writes and every member sees it) and the studio export re-verified after the extraction.
