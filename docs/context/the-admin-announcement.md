## The admin announcement — a banner for when something is wrong (2026-09-26, owner's request)
**A message the owner types on `/admin` and every reader sees on the screener.** Stored in `app_meta` under `announcement` beside `hidden_columns` and the rest, because it is a site setting rather than anyone's preference.

- **THE SCREENER ONLY, and that was the owner's choice against my recommendation.** Offered "everywhere including signed-out" (which reaches people who cannot get in, when sign-in is the broken thing) and "signed-in pages only"; the owner took the smallest build. **It therefore rides `GET /api/prefs`** — the call the screener already awaits before its first render, the same channel `siteHidden` and `tileConfig` use — so there is **no new endpoint on the read path and no new script tag**. Worth knowing if it is ever widened: only `index.html` reads prefs, so any other page needs its own fetch, and `wmark.js` is the established one-tag-per-page pattern for that.
- **A GUEST SEES IT TOO.** If the data is wrong it is wrong on the preview as well, and somebody deciding whether to sign up should not be the only person not told.
- **`at` IS THE VERSION, and it is the whole reason the stored shape is not a bare string.** Dismissal is per device in `localStorage`, keyed on that timestamp — so an × hides it, and **editing the message brings it back for everyone**, which is what makes a follow-up ("fixed now") reach exactly the people who dismissed the first one. The owner picked this over both never-dismissible and dismissed-forever.
- **Clearing stores `null`, never an empty string.** *Nothing to say* and *something to say, which is blank* must not be two states a reader can land in.
- **The level IS the colour, and the colours already mean these things here**: accent is a fact, amber is *notice this* (the Price pulled column, the Turso bar, the stale status pill), red is the loud cell. The console's level chips are painted in the colour they will produce, so the choice is previewed rather than described.
- **Every visibility change calls `paintWindow(true)`** — the news ticker's and the phone bar's rule. The page never scrolls, the table sizes itself from `.table-wrap`, and **no resize event fires when a sibling appears**; without it the last rows fall off the bottom. Measured: the wrap goes 681 → 752px when the banner is dismissed.
- **Written only by `PUT /api/announcement` (admin), deliberately not through the prefs PUT** — that endpoint rebuilds a per-user row from an allowlist, and a site-wide message has no business travelling with somebody's collapsed columns. The activity log records the LEVEL, never the text: facts, not content.
- **The editor is inline at the top of `/admin`, above every section** — this is what you reach for when something is wrong, and a link to another page is a click paid at the worst moment. It also means the console's first line tells you whether a banner is up right now, which the textarea alone could not (a draft and a live message look identical).
- Verified: **45 checks** — the store (whitespace collapsed so a pasted newline is not a layout bug, the cap, an unknown level falling back, clearing leaving nothing), member/guest/stranger all refused on both routes, a member AND a guest receiving it through prefs, the banner drawn at the right colour measured off the icon, **dismissal surviving a reload and then an edit bringing it back**, markup in the message rendered as text, the table reclaiming its height, and the console panel saving, reporting and clearing.
- **`esc` does not exist in `admin.html`** — the first cut of the panel called it and would have shipped a `ReferenceError`. The status line is built with `textContent` and `append` instead, rather than inventing a second escaper on a page that has none.
