# The Stock Signal card (2026-10-29)

The owner: "Under promo, Can you create a new card under Stock Spotlight called Stock Signal, It shoud show the signal details and possibly the reason for it".

- **Template `stksignal`**, second in the Stock spotlight group (`SPOT_VIEWS`, after Overview). One control, `sigSym` (the stock; in `CONTROL_IDS`, its own change listener, filled from `chtSym` like the other stock pickers, box `sigBox`).
- **What it draws**: the Signal word large in its tint, "because «rule» fired first", the rulebook (company type) and how long it has read this way (`adviceDays`, `adviceSince`) or "Changed today, from …" (`advicePrev`); the fired rule's conditions, one row each with the stock's value, a gauge (the scale, the passing zone shaded, a dot at the value) and a filled or hollow mark for met; "any one of" groups indented; and the four readings behind it (Trend, Entry, Fundamentals, Guards), each the label its own ladder lit with the conditions that lit it. A default (fallback) rule is explained in words instead of conditions. A fundamentals bucket of none reads **"No case either way"**, not a dash (the question about Alphabet's "—" on the screener). A fund has no Fundamentals reading.
- **One engine**: `ActionRules.explain(row, ActionRules.resolve({}).cfg)` over the row the host holds, the call the stock page and the intro card's ladder make. The studio's rows are `/api/stocks`'s and `/api/m/post` scores the snapshot through `scoreActionInto`, so both carry the source rule's figures (`data-sources.md`) and the card reads the same Signal as the screener. Rule names go through `ActionRules.canonFlag`, the word through `canon`.
- **Colour**: only the word and its hero carry the verdict's tint; gauges and marks are the accent; no green or red elsewhere (checked). Values print a minus sign, thresholds in the labels too.
- **Note**: a mechanical reading of strength from the Balanced rule set, not an instruction to trade; the first matching rule decides; the conditions are that rule's with the stock's values.
- Test (scratchpad `stksignal-test.js`, uni-boot over a copy of production's snapshot): offered and its picker shown; one stock per Signal word (APTV, GM, PDD, SMH, NTAP, SPOT), the wordiest rule (TSM, five conditions), the default reading (TSLA) and the longest name (BEPC) at all three sizes: the engine's word and rule, nothing past the artboard, the note clear of the footer, no green or red outside the word; a fund without a fundamentals reading; none read as words; a saved post drawn by the server with the same word. 96 checks.
